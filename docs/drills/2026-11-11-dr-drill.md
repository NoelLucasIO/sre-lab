## What went well

- The pipeline rebuilt the infrastructure in 2.5 minutes, and both approval gates held.
- The restore was exact (9/9 collections) and took one minute; the throwaway-Mongo proof ran before anything on k3s was touched.
- The SSO session was enough: the restore ran at T0 +18 with no re-login.
- Alerting worked as designed: a page at T0 +4, a ticket for the blind exporter, and the ticket auto-resolved once the exporter was back.
- The `drill/mark` log made the timings a matter of record, not memory.
